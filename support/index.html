Signing in

Traksy doesn't use passwords. Enter your phone number or email address, and we'll send you a one-time code by text or email - enter that code in the app to sign in. If you don't receive a code within a minute or two, check that you entered your phone number or email correctly, then try "Resend code" on the code screen.

The first time you sign in, you'll either create a new organization for your business or, if someone already invited you, join theirs automatically.

Scanning receipts

Tap the camera button and take a photo of a receipt, or choose one from your photo library. Traksy reads the vendor, date, payment method, and line items automatically - review the results, make any corrections, and save. You can also add items manually without a photo for cash purchases or receipts you don't have on hand.

Every receipt is recorded against whichever location is selected at the top of the app, so make sure the right location is picked before you save.

Team and locations

Owners and admins can add locations, invite team members, and assign each person a role - Owner, Admin, or Member. Members can be scoped to a single location and only see their own uploads; Owners and Admins see everything across every location. Staff records (pay info, employment type) can be added with or without giving that person app access.

Reports and StockIQ

The Reports tab shows your top stores, spend by category, and most-purchased items over any date range, plus a search for exactly how much you've spent on a specific item. StockIQ estimates what you likely have on hand for frequently repurchased items, based on how often you've historically rebought them - it's an estimate, not an exact count.

Troubleshooting

  • Didn't receive a sign-in code: double-check the phone number or email, then use "Resend code." Codes expire after a few minutes.
  • A receipt won't save: make sure every included item has a price, and that you have a network connection.
  • Missing a tab or option: some tabs (Reports, Fixed Costs, Team) are only available to Owners and Admins, not Members.
  • Wrong totals or items: open the receipt and edit any item directly - scanned totals are a starting point, not final.

Contact us

Still need help? Email support@tracksy.app and include your organization name and a short description of the issue - we typically reply within one business day.
