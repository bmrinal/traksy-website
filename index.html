How it works

From receipt photo to decision-ready records.

The app turns purchase capture into a short review loop, so operators keep control of what gets saved while Traksy handles the repetitive parsing and categorization.

01

Capture the receipt

Use the camera or photo library. Traksy reads vendor, date, payment method, and line items from the image.

02

Review every line

Items are selected by default. Adjust categories, leave out noise, fix the vendor, and save only what matters.

03

See the operation

Filter receipts, compare item spend, watch category totals, and track purchase cadence by location.

Operating coverage

One mobile workspace for the records operators actually revisit.

Receipt archive

Browse, filter, edit, and delete receipt records with vendor, date, payment method, item count, and totals.

Item reports

Search any recorded item to see total spend, quantity, and receipt count over custom date ranges.

StockIQ

Estimate remaining inventory from repeat purchase cadence, normalized product names, and confidence bands.

Shopping lists

Build today's list by location and keep a seven-day read-only history of what was checked off.

Staff costs

Track pay type, pay rate, contact details, active status, final settlements, and monthly cost history.

Fixed expenses

Record recurring costs such as rent, internet, phone, or utilities by week, month, year, and location.

Locations

Group records by physical location, set a default view, and scope members to one location when needed.

Team roles

Owners and admins manage reports and settings while members stay focused on location-limited receipt work.

Trust model

Designed around review, scoping, and private storage.

Traksy stores receipt records against an organization, keeps original receipt images private, uses short-lived image links when a user requests a receipt photo, and lets roles limit what each person can access.

Unconfirmed scans expire Temporary receipt photos are set to expire after one day when they are not saved.
Passwordless sign-in Users authenticate through phone or email one-time codes.
Organization boundaries Receipts, items, staff, costs, and lists are stored per organization and optionally per location.
