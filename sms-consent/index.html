SMS opt-in

How people agree to Traksy text messages.

Traksy only texts one-time sign-in codes, and only after a person enters their own mobile number in the Traksy app and ticks an SMS consent box. This page shows that flow, screen by screen.

1. Opt-in flow in the Traksy app

Screenshots from the Traksy iOS app - tap any one for the full-size, uncropped original. Phone numbers and codes are blurred for privacy.

Step 1 - Open the app and choose Phone. The sign-in screen asks for a phone number or an email address (choosing email sends no text messages at all). With Phone selected, the SMS consent checkbox appears unchecked by default, with links to the Terms and Privacy Policy right beside it, and Continue is disabled.

Step 2 - Enter a mobile number. Entering a number alone isn't enough: the Continue button stays disabled until the consent box is ticked.

Step 3 - Tick the box. Only once the person ticks the consent box can they continue.

Step 4 - Receive the code. Tapping Continue sends exactly one text containing the sign-in code, which they enter to sign in.

2. Consent wording

The checkbox reads, word for word:

☐ I agree to receive one-time sign-in codes by text message from Traksy. One message per code requested. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Terms · Privacy Policy

3. What we send

Only one-time sign-in codes - one message each time a person requests a code. No marketing or promotional messages. Example: "123456 is your verification code for Traksy".

4. Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any Traksy message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. Support: support@traksy.app. Mobile numbers and SMS consent are never shared with third parties for marketing - see the Privacy Policy.

5. Other entry points

Changing a phone number (Settings) shows the same unchecked consent checkbox before the verification code is sent. Team invitations by a business owner send no text message; the invited person receives a code only after they open the app and complete the opt-in above themselves.
