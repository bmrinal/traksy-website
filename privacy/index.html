Privacy policy

How Traksy handles business records.

This policy explains the information Traksy collects, how it is used, and how receipt photos, item data, team data, staff records, locations, costs, and shopping lists are handled.

Last updated August 31, 2026

Questions: privacy@tracksy.app

Overview Information collected How information is used Service providers Storage and retention Sharing Choices and rights Security Contact

1. Overview

Traksy helps organizations capture purchase receipts, review item-level details, understand spending, estimate inventory patterns, manage shopping lists, record fixed costs, and maintain staff and team information. Information is organized by organization and, when selected, by business location.

2. Information collected

Depending on how Traksy is used, the app may collect or create:

  • Account identifiers such as phone number, email address, authentication user ID, and one-time-code login metadata.
  • Profile and organization details such as name, business name, membership status, role, invitations, and default location.
  • Location details such as location name and optional address.
  • Receipt data such as receipt photos, vendor, vendor domain, purchase date, payment method, item names, quantities, categories, prices, totals, and timestamps.
  • Operational records such as shopping list items, fixed costs, staff names, contact details, employment type, pay type, pay rate, status, and settlement details.
  • Technical records such as API requests, diagnostic logs, authentication events, and raw AI parsing responses used for debugging and reliability.

3. How information is used

Traksy uses information to:

  • Authenticate users through passwordless phone or email sign-in.
  • Create and manage organizations, locations, memberships, roles, and invitations.
  • Extract receipt details from photos and let users review, edit, save, and filter receipt records.
  • Generate item, category, and date-range reports for owners and admins.
  • Estimate inventory signals through StockIQ based on repeat purchase patterns and normalized product names.
  • Maintain shopping lists, fixed costs, staff records, staff cost history, and organization settings.
  • Monitor, secure, debug, and improve the app and backend services.

4. Service providers

Traksy relies on trusted infrastructure and service providers to operate the app. Authentication is handled through Auth0. Backend services use AWS infrastructure, including API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB, S3, and CloudWatch. Receipt photos and some item names may be processed by OpenAI services to extract receipt fields, infer categories, and normalize inventory item names.

These providers process information only as needed to provide the app, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, or comply with legal obligations.

5. Storage and retention

Receipt photos are stored in private cloud storage. Unconfirmed temporary receipt scans are configured to expire after one day. When a user confirms and saves a receipt, the receipt image may be copied into permanent receipt storage and retained with the receipt record unless it is deleted or the organization requests removal.

Organization records, receipt records, items, staff data, fixed costs, categories, memberships, and shopping lists are retained while they are needed to provide Traksy or meet operational, security, and legal requirements. Older shopping list rows may be pruned after the app's short history window.

6. Sharing

Traksy does not sell personal information. Information may be visible to authorized users inside the same organization according to their role and location scope. Owners and admins may have access to broader reporting, team, category, staff, fixed-cost, and location settings than members.

Information may also be shared with service providers, legal authorities, or professional advisors when necessary to provide the app, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, comply with law, or complete a business transfer.

7. Choices and rights

Users can edit many records directly in the app, including receipt details, categories, locations, team member names, staff records, and fixed costs, depending on role permissions. Users or organization owners may request access, correction, export, or deletion of information by contacting Traksy.

Some information may need to be retained for security, troubleshooting, accounting, backup, legal, or legitimate business reasons.

8. Security

Traksy uses passwordless authentication, token-protected API access, private receipt-image storage, server-side receipt processing, encrypted cloud storage configuration, and role-based access patterns. No system is perfectly secure, so users should protect access to their devices, email accounts, and phone numbers used for one-time-code login.

9. Children

Traksy is intended for business use and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided information to Traksy, contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

10. Changes

Traksy may update this policy as the app, providers, or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of the page shows when the policy was last revised.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact privacy@tracksy.app.
